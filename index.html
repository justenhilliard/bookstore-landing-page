Our Story

Tree Stump Bookstore is a magical little bookshop built on the idea that stories take root. Tucked into an old historic building and inspired by woodland folklore, the store feels like a place where forgotten tales, handwritten notes, and well-loved books have been waiting for the right person to find them.

Inside, you'll find shelves of classics, folklore, poetry, and rare little gems, each chosen to make the space feel thoughtful, timeless, and full of quiet wonder. More than a bookshop, Tree Stump is a place to slow down, get lost for a while, and leave with a story that stays with you

Some places sell books. Others keep stories alive.
